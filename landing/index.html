Honest answers

Is it as good as a real engineer?

For most releases — yes. It nails the things that actually matter: loudness, tone balance, and true-peak safety, set to streaming standards. A top engineer still wins on big creative calls, but for the price and speed, nothing comes close.

Do you keep or sell my music?

Never. Your music is 100% yours. Files are processed and then deleted — we don't sell them, share them, or train anything on them. That's a promise, not fine print.

Will it sound distorted or too loud?

No. Every master is true-peak limited, so it's loud and clean — never crunchy or clipped.

What files can I use?

WAV, MP3, FLAC in. WAV or MP3 out.
